Digital identity for Creative Europe Luxembourg

Creative Europe serves as the European Commission's comprehensive funding framework designed to bolster cultural and creative industries across the region. As part of my permanent position as a ui designer at custom coding, I designed the complete digital identity for Creative Europe Luxembourg, taking the project from initial branding concepts through to final user interface implementation. The work began with developing a cohesive brand strategy that honored the European Commission's Creative Europe framework while establishing a distinct identity for the Luxembourg branch.

Streamlining Creative Funding Through Strategic Interface Design

The UI implementation focused on creating an information architecture that simplified complex funding processes. Interactive elements guided users through application procedures, while dynamic content areas showcased successful projects and community achievements. The final interface balanced professional functionality with creative inspiration, reflecting Luxembourg's position as both a financial hub and cultural center within the European creative landscape. The revision explains how the UI was designed to accommodate the distinct needs of each sector while maintaining a cohesive user experience across film and audiovisual productions, cultural exchanges, and cross-sector collaborations.
