Education Report Luxembourg - Website Design

Luxembourg's educational insights with clarity and visual impact.

As part of my permanent position as a ui designer at custom coding, I designed the Educational Report Luxembourg website, while working for Custom Coding. I was using Figma, creating a clean and accessible platform that makes complex educational data easy to navigate and understand. Working closely with the development team, I ensured the design translated seamlessly from concept to code, delivering a user-friendly experience that effectively communicates Luxembourg's educational insights to diverse audiences.
