SICA App

Streamlined Waste Management and Community Services

As part of my permanent position as a UI designer at custom coding, I created the SICA App - a comprehensive digital platform that serves residents across various communities in western Luxembourg by streamlining essential municipal services through an intuitive, centralized interface for waste management and community communication.

Never Miss Collection Day Again

Through the app, residents can easily register for garbage pickup services, ensuring their waste collection needs are properly coordinated with local authorities. The integrated garbage calendar feature keeps users informed about collection schedules, helping them stay organized and never miss a pickup day. Additionally, the app functions as a vital communication channel, delivering important community news and announcements directly to residents' mobile devices.
