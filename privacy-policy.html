Hosting

This website is hosted by Framer B.V., Johan Huizingalaan 763a, 1066 VH Amsterdam, The Netherlands. When you visit our website, the hosting provider automatically collects data (“server log files”) that your browser transmits. This includes:

  • Visited pages on our domain
  • Date and time of the request
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL
  • Hostname of the accessing device
  • IP address

This data is collected to ensure a smooth and secure operation of the website and for statistical analysis to improve the service. This data is not combined with other data sources.

Framer hosts content within the EU and complies with the GDPR. For more information, see Framer’s Privacy Policy.

This website uses Google Fonts, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

Cookies and Consent

This website does not use cookies that process personal data or track user behavior. The only item it stores is mb_consent, a first-party cookie that records your cookie choice for 12 months. If your browser blocks cookies, the same information is kept in your browser’s local storage instead. It is strictly necessary to remember your decision.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using “Cookie Settings” in the footer.
